## Top 3 Headlines

### 1. ⚡ Instagram Expands Teen Accounts with 13+ Content Ratings Globally
Meta announced the international expansion of its age-appropriate 13+ content rating and "Limited Content" setting for Teen Accounts, building on the US/UK/Australia/Canada rollout from October 2025. Teens under 18 are now automatically enrolled and cannot opt out without parental permission. Content filtering mirrors movie-style PG-13 standards.  
**Source:** [Meta Newsroom](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/04/instagram-expands-teen-accounts-inspired-by-13-content-ratings/) | [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/09/instagram-expands-its-movie-inspired-content-restriction-for-teens-internationally/)

### 2. COPPA Amended Rule Compliance Deadline Approaching (April 22)
Companies have until **April 22, 2026** to comply with the FTC's amended COPPA Rule, which expands the definition of personal information to include biometric identifiers, enhances notice requirements, mandates written information security programs, and imposes stricter data retention limits. The FTC also issued a February 2026 policy statement encouraging age verification tech adoption.  
**Source:** [BBB National Programs](https://bbbprograms.org/media/insights/blog/coppa-amended) | [Toy Association](https://www.toyassociation.org/PressRoom2/News/2026-News/updated-coppa-rule-requirements-take-effect-april-22.aspx)

### 3. Congressional Efforts on Kids Safety Stall as KOSA Fight Continues
The House version of the Kids Online Safety Act (embedded in the KIDS Act) advanced to the full House floor but with the critical "duty of care" provision gutted. Meanwhile, Rep. Gottheimer introduced the bipartisan "Parents Decide Act" on April 2 requiring OS-level age verification. Intensive tech industry lobbying continues to slow progress.  
**Source:** [Spectrum News](https://spectrumlocalnews.com/us/snplus/news/2026/04/10/congress-stalls-social-media-legislation) | [Rep. Gottheimer](https://gottheimer.house.gov/posts/release-gottheimer-announces-bipartisan-parents-decide-act-to-protect-kids-online)

## Competitor Watch

### ⚡ TikTok
- **Family Pairing**: 15+ adjustable settings; parents can see followers/following, block accounts, set custom screen time by day of week, enable STEM feed
- **Policy Changes (Jan 22, 2026)**: New ToS under US-based ownership; now collects precise location data (previously prohibited for US users); privacy policy backlash ongoing
- **Teen Defaults**: Private accounts, 60-min daily screen time, restricted DMs from strangers
- **Livestreaming**: Min age 16 to host, 18 to receive gifts; 1,000 follower minimum
- No new Family Pairing feature announcements this week

### Meta / Instagram
- **⚡ 13+ Content Rating Expansion (Apr 9, 2026)**: Global rollout of movie-style content standards for teens; mandatory enrollment; opt-out requires parental permission
- **Limited Content mode**: New stricter option for parents
- **Age verification**: AI proactively detects teens using adult birthdays; selfie video + ID verification
- **$375M verdict**: New Mexico jury ordered Meta to pay for child exploitation failures (Mar 24, 2026) — appeal pending

### YouTube / Google
- **Family Link**: Supervised experiences with Explore/Explore More/Most of YouTube tiers
- **Shorts limit**: Parents can now set daily Shorts feed to zero (Mar 2026)
- **Supervision ends at 13**: Auto-graduation to standard account option
- **Under investigation**: Australia eSafety Commissioner examining underage access

### Snapchat
- **Family Center**: Active parental controls
- **Under investigation**: Australia eSafety examining compliance with under-16 ban
- **EU**: European Commission sent requests for information regarding minor safeguards

### Discord
- **Teen-by-default settings**: Rolled out Mar 2026; restricted mode for under-18
- **Family Center update**: Parents can monitor purchases, top interactions, time spent
- **Guardian-locked controls**: DM settings and sensitive content filters controlled by parents
- **Lawsuits**: Ongoing litigation for teen harm

### Roblox
- **Age verification**: Mandatory ID/facial scan for 18+ content and chat (Jan 1, 2026)
- **Under-9 restrictions**: Chat disabled by default unless parent-verified
- **Under-13**: No DMs outside active games
- **Iowa lawsuit**: State sued Roblox for deceptive marketing re: child safety
- **146 lawsuits**: Federal MDL as of Apr 2026

### Apple & Google (OS/Store)
- **Apple**: New 13+, 16+, 18+ ratings; Declared Age Range API live Jan 2026; expanded teen protections (web filters, Communication Safety)
- **Google**: Play Age Signals API; Family Link parental controls
- **Texas mandate**: App stores must verify age 18+ or parental approval before download (effective Jan 2026)

### X (Twitter)
- No supervised parental controls or Family Center equivalent
- No screen time controls
- No new child safety announcements

### BeReal
- No parental controls available
- No new child safety announcements

### Pinterest
