📰 Top Headlines

⚡ Instagram Expands Teen Accounts with 13+ Content Ratings Globally

Meta announced international expansion of age-appropriate 13+ content rating and "Limited Content" setting. Teens under 18 auto-enrolled; opt-out requires parental permission. Content filtering mirrors movie-style PG-13 standards across feeds, search, and recommendations.

Meta Newsroom ↗ TechCrunch ↗

COPPA Amended Rule Compliance Deadline: April 22

Companies have until April 22, 2026 to comply with the FTC's amended COPPA Rule — expanded definition of personal information (now includes biometrics), enhanced notice requirements, mandatory written infosec programs, stricter data retention limits.

BBB National Programs ↗

Congressional Efforts on Kids Safety Stall

KOSA's "duty of care" provision gutted in House version (KIDS Act). Rep. Gottheimer introduced bipartisan "Parents Decide Act" (Apr 2) requiring OS-level age verification. Tech industry lobbying continues to slow progress.
